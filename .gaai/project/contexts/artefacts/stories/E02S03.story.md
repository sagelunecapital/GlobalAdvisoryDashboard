# Story E02S03: Compute YoY% Change and 3-Month Moving Average

## User Story

**As a** fund manager,
**I want** to see the year-over-year percentage change and its 3-month moving average for the key liquidity series,
**so that** I can assess the trend and direction of liquidity conditions beyond the raw level, which can be misleading in isolation.

---

## Context

Three series require YoY% and 3MA computation: WRESBAL, Net Liquidity (from E02S02), and BOPGSTB. YoY% is computed as ((current − prior_year_value) / |prior_year_value|) × 100 — where prior-year value is the observation 52 weeks prior for weekly series (WRESBAL, Net Liquidity) and the observation 12 months prior for the monthly series (BOPGSTB). The 3-month moving average smooths the YoY% series to reduce noise. Results are stored as derived series in the database, available for direct querying by the display layer.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: Given WRESBAL, Net Liquidity, and BOPGSTB data are available in the database, the system computes the YoY% change for each series at each available date where a prior-year value exists (52 weeks prior for WRESBAL and Net Liquidity; 12 months prior for BOPGSTB), and persists each as a named derived series (e.g., `WRESBAL_YOY`, `NET_LIQUIDITY_YOY`, `BOPGSTB_YOY`)
- [ ] AC2: Given the YoY% series are computed, the system computes a 3-month moving average — defined as a 13-week simple rolling average for weekly series (WRESBAL, Net Liquidity) and a 3-point simple rolling average for the monthly series (BOPGSTB) — and persists each as a named derived series (e.g., `WRESBAL_YOY_3MA`, `NET_LIQUIDITY_YOY_3MA`, `BOPGSTB_YOY_3MA`)
- [ ] AC3: Given a date where the prior-year value is unavailable (within the first prior-year look-back window: 52 weeks for WRESBAL and Net Liquidity, 12 months for BOPGSTB), that date is excluded from the YoY% series — no computation is attempted with a null denominator
- [ ] AC4: Given a date where fewer than the required number of preceding YoY% values exist for the 3MA window, that date is excluded from the 3MA series
- [ ] AC5: Given new raw data is appended by E02S01/E02S02, only the new date range is recomputed and appended to the derived series — previously computed values are not recalculated
- [ ] AC6 (error-handling): Given the underlying raw series is empty or corrupt for a given series, the system logs a warning for that series and continues computing the remaining series — one failure does not block the others
- [ ] AC7 (data-freshness): Each stored derived record includes the computation timestamp

## Notes

BOPGSTB is monthly; its YoY% uses a 12-month look-back and its 3MA uses a 3-point (3-month) rolling average. WRESBAL and Net Liquidity are weekly; their YoY% uses a 52-week look-back and their 3MA uses a 13-week rolling average. 13 weeks is the standard fixed-count approximation for 3 calendar months applied to weekly data. Delivery should use these window definitions consistently across all three series.

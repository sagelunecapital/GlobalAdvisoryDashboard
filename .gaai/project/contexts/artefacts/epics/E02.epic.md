# Epic E02: Liquidity Module

## Purpose

Fund managers can view the current state of market liquidity through four key series (Federal Reserve balance sheet net liquidity, reserve balances, trade balance, and Bitcoin price), both in raw form and as year-over-year percentage change with smoothing — giving them a clear read on whether liquidity conditions are expanding or contracting without manual data aggregation.

---

## Scope

- Fetch and persist FRED liquidity data (WRESBAL, WALCL, WDTGAL, WLRRAL, BOPGSTB) and Bitcoin price from 2020 onward; weekly and monthly frequency
- Compute net liquidity series: WALCL − WDTGAL − WLRRAL
- Compute YoY% change and 3-month moving average for WRESBAL, Net Liquidity, and BOPGSTB
- Display Section A: tabbed raw charts (one tab per series — WRESBAL, Net Liquidity, BOPGSTB, Bitcoin)
- Display Section B: three separate YoY% 3MA charts (WRESBAL, Net Liquidity, BOPGSTB)
- Manual on-demand data refresh (scheduled refresh is a future enhancement)

---

## Out of Scope

- Cross-module signal alignment with Macro or Market Regime modules (future Epic)
- Intraday or tick-level data — weekly and monthly only
- Alerts or push notifications
- User-configurable indicator selection
- Non-FRED liquidity proxies beyond the specified series

---

## Success Metrics

- All five FRED series and Bitcoin price fetched correctly and stored from 2020 onward
- Net liquidity computed accurately (WALCL − WDTGAL − WLRRAL) and updated with each refresh
- YoY% and 3MA charts render correctly for the three applicable series
- Fund managers can switch between raw and derived views in under 2 seconds
- Error states are explicit — no silent failures or stale data displayed as current

---

## Dependencies

None — Liquidity module is independent of E01 (Market Regime). Both modules share the same product context but have separate data pipelines.

# Epic E03: Macro Module

## Purpose

Fund managers can monitor 32 macroeconomic indicators across five categories (Inflation, Yields, Currencies, Futures, Growth) in a single view — seeing current values, weekly % change, and rolling standard deviation — and receive natural-language signal alerts when any indicator moves more than two standard deviations from its 52-week baseline, so they can identify macro stress or inflection points without manual calculation.

---

## Scope

- Fetch and persist 32 macro indicators from Barcharts, Yahoo Finance, and the Atlanta Fed GDPNow feed; weekly frequency
- Compute weekly % change and 52-week rolling standard deviation per indicator
- Detect 2σ threshold breaches; generate a natural-language reason for each alert
- Display all indicators organized by category: current value, % change, std dev, and signal alerts
- Manual and/or scheduled data refresh

---

## Out of Scope

- Cross-module signal alignment with Liquidity or Market Regime (future Epic)
- Historical charting of individual indicators (display is a current-value table, not a time-series chart)
- Custom indicator addition by the user
- Sub-weekly (daily, intraday) frequency for Macro indicators
- Alerts or push notifications (display only)

---

## Stories

> Stories are tracked in the backlog. This list is descriptive only.

- E03S02: Compute weekly % change and rolling 52-week standard deviation
- E03S03: Detect 2σ signals and generate natural-language alert reasons
- E03S04: Display Macro module (categorized table with signal alerts)

---

## Success Metrics

- All 32 indicators fetched correctly and stored at weekly frequency
- % change and std dev computed correctly for each indicator
- 2σ signals trigger accurately and produce readable natural-language reasons
- Fund managers can scan the full macro dashboard in a single scroll with signal alerts visually prominent
- Error states are explicit — partial data is surfaced, not silently omitted

---

## Dependencies

None — Macro module is independent of E01 and E02. All three modules share the same product context but have separate data pipelines.

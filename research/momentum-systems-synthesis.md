## 0. The Shared DNA ⚪

All three sit in the same **US growth-leadership / trend-momentum** school. Common ancestors:

| Ancestor | What each system inherits |
|---|---|
| **O'Neil (CANSLIM)** | Buy market-leading growth stocks with earnings/sales acceleration, near new highs, with volume confirmation; cut losses ~7–8%. |
| **Minervini (SEPA + Trend Template)** | Trade only Stage-2 uptrends; demand the trend template (price > 50 > 150 > 200 DMA, 200 rising, RS line at highs); buy volatility-contraction setups; risk-first sizing. |
| **Weinstein (Stage Analysis)** | The 4-stage cycle (1 basing → 2 advancing → 3 topping → 4 declining); only buy Stage 2, exit on Stage 3 / break of the 30-week MA. |

Shared creed: **trade leaders only, in confirmed uptrends, with tightly defined risk, and let winners run.** The divergence is *how you get in* (breakout vs. contraction/pullback) and *how mechanical vs. discretionary* the rules are.

## 2. Side-by-Side

### Where they overlap (consensus core)
1. **Leaders only, via RS.** All three screen for relative strength to isolate leadership.
2. **Stage-2 / uptrend gating.** All trade only confirmed advances — none bottom-fish.
3. **Risk-first sizing.** Risk defined *before* entry; size derived from stop distance.
4. **Asymmetry — cut fast, let winners run.**
5. **Volatility-contraction lineage.** Prime's "cheat", Komar's VCP, Caruso's coil all descend from Minervini's VCP.
6. **Buy contraction, not extension (2 of 3).** Prime *and* Caruso explicitly avoid chasing breakouts; both buy low-risk entries into contraction/support.

### Where they genuinely contradict

| Axis | Prime Trading | Komar | Caruso |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entry bias** | Pullback to 21-DMA; **no breakouts** | **Both** breakouts & pullbacks (4 types) | Low-risk **contraction** entry (mini-coil); avoids breakouts |
| **Fundamentals** | Not a hard gate (RS/liquidity) | **Hard gate** | **Hard gate** (screened first) |
| **Risk per trade** | Tiered 0.25 / 0.5 / 1% by conviction | Fixed ~1%, 5% portfolio cap | Per-setup invalidation level |
| **Primary signal** | 21-DMA structure | Price > fundamentals > story | RS + institutional accumulation |
| **Discretion** | Highly mechanical (counter + Z-score + R-rules) | Most discretionary (3 lenses align) | Mechanical entry/stop, discretionary setup read |

**The real fault line:** *Komar (will buy breakouts, broadest, fundamentals-gated)* vs. *Prime + Caruso (both buy contraction/low-risk entries, avoid chasing)*. Prime is the lone outlier on fundamentals (RS/liquidity only); the other two gate hard on earnings growth.

## 3. Synthesized "Best-of" Framework

**1. Universe & screen (Komar/Caruso rigor + all-three RS)**
- RS ≥ 90 / RS line at-or-near new highs.
- Minervini trend template (price > 50 > 150 > 200 DMA, 200 rising).
- Fundamental gate: accelerating sales; positive/growing EPS for mature names; relax only for genuine early-stage leaders.

**2. Setup (Caruso tightness + Prime structure)**
- Prefer names that have formed a **tight coil/VCP** *and* sit on a **rising 21-DMA structure** — consolidation at support, not extended.

**3. Entry — dual-mode by conviction & tape**
- **Pullback mode (Prime):** within **1× ATR of the 21-DMA**; weakness entry into support for best R, or wait for 21-DMA reclaim / tight-range reversal for confirmation.
- **Contraction-break mode (Caruso):** buy the break out of the coil on volume, stop at low of final coil bar.
- **Always scale in (Komar):** small starter, add on confirmation.

**4. Risk & sizing (consensus)**
- **1% max risk per trade**; size = (1% × equity) ÷ stop distance.
- **Portfolio heat ≤ 5%** total open risk (Komar's cap).
- Trim/reduce into earnings.

**5. Exits (Prime's R-engine)**
- Sell **⅓ at 2R** to bank profit and drop net risk ~0.
- Hold the runner to a **21-DMA-structure break on a daily close.**
- Use the **cycle counter + Z-score** as a portfolio-wide extension governor: tighten / avoid new adds when leaders hit +3σ on time-above-structure.

**Synthesis logic:** Komar supplies the *quality filter*; Caruso the *tightest setup geometry*; Prime the *mechanical entry-zone discipline + R-based exit engine*. The 21-DMA structure is the common spine tying entry, runner-management, and extension-risk together.
